Resus Room

By Kim Trinh, MD · for iPhone, iPad and Mac

Resus Room puts you in charge of the resuscitation room. The monitor is live, the team is waiting, and the patient in the bed needs you now. Give your orders out loud or with a tap — “Start compressions”, “Charge to 200”, “Epinephrine 1 milligram” — and the nurse answers, the rhythm changes, and the clock keeps running.

Fourteen emergencies built by an emergency physician: a collapse on a casino floor, adult cardiac arrests and rhythm emergencies, major trauma, children, and newborns. Eight are free; one purchase unlocks the rest. No account, no ads, and your cases stay on your device.

For education and simulation practice only. Resus Room is not medical advice, is not a certification, and must not be used for decisions about real patients. The patients are fictional.
